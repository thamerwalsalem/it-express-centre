## Information architecture rules
- Homepage: concise introduction; clear 4 primary entry cards (Individuals, Business, Smart Home, Build Your Idea); prominent AI Transformation and Store entry points; shared assistant and technician signup.
- Services grouped in one menu; do not overwhelm homepage with every service.
- One canonical service record with localized Arabic and English labels, descriptions, routes, FAQs and actions.
- Changing language preserves page/service and context; no redirect to homepage.
- Section accent color changes across calls to action, highlights and icons, but the logo shape, typography and layout stay invariant.
- Services are catalog-driven; new subservices can be added without changing global navigation.
- All request forms require real backend validation, authenticated storage, notifications and privacy notices before marked operational.
- Clearly distinguish active services from coming-soon offerings. No unverified promises of 24/7 or worldwide availability.
- Commerce, booking, AI agent and login need backend integrations; static HTML alone is not enough.
- Draft branch only; workflow auto-deploy configured on main HTML changes. No publication before explicit owner approval.
## Proposed release phases

5. Store, payments and partner fulfillment after contractual/legal checks.
